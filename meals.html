Meal plan

14 days of meals and snacks, the recipes, the shopping list. Eat every 2 to 4 hours: lean protein, a healthy carb, a vegetable, and a healthy fat in a couple of meals a day.

The rules of the road

This challenge, skip sugar, dairy as a snack, wheat and gluten, and fried or pre-packaged food. Every meal has a lean protein, a healthy carb and a vegetable. A healthy fat goes in at least two meals a day. Eat before you get hungry: a small meal or snack every 2 to 4 hours keeps blood sugar steady, which is what keeps nausea, fatigue and cravings down.
