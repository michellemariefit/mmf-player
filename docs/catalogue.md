## Sales page promises vs. members area
- "Workouts start on week 2 after the 7 Day Healthy Habits Jumpstart, 5 workouts a week for 2 weeks" (FAQ) vs. the course: habits are Week 1 lessons titled Day 1 to 7 and there are three weeks of workouts (15 workouts).
- "Meal Planning & Prepping Hacks Video" is promised; there is a Meal Prepping video in Dropbox (YOUTUBE VIDEOS/UPLOADED, 2018) but no meal prep lesson in the course.
- "Diastasis recti bonus that shows how to prevent it getting worse" (FAQ 18) is not in the course. The 2018 recordings exist in Dropbox.
- "Personal meal plan" is a 2016 freebie (Pregnancy Without The Pounds) with 2016 design.
- Day 2 lesson promises coupon codes unlocked as you go; the codes are the same 50OFF on nearly every lesson.
