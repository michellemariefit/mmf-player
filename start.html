Three ways to use a workout

Play walks you through one move at a time and runs the timers for you. Best on a phone, mid-workout.

List shows the whole workout on one page with the start and end photos. Tick rounds as you go.

Print gives you one page per workout, photos included. Stick it on the fridge.

Put it on your home screen

Then it opens like an app, full screen, and the photos load even with bad signal.

  1. iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow), then "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
  2. Android: open this page in Chrome. Tap the three dots at the top right, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
